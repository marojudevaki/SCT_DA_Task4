
BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT – MARKETING CAMPAIGN ANALYSIS

Objective:
This analysis was performed to identify which marketing channels generate the
best business returns and should receive more budget.

Data Preparation:
The marketing campaign dataset contained 120 records and 20 columns.
Missing-value and duplicate checks found no missing values and no duplicate rows.
Start_Date and End_Date were converted into proper datetime format.

Funnel Analysis:
The campaigns generated 73,796,970 impressions, 2,324,166 clicks,
172,913 leads, and 42,551 conversions. The overall conversion from
impressions to conversions was approximately 0.06%. The click rate was
approximately 3.15%, the lead rate from clicks was 7.44%, and the conversion
rate from leads was 24.61%.

Channel Performance:
Email was the strongest-performing channel based on calculated ROI.
It generated revenue of approximately $5.07 million from spending of
approximately $690.64 thousand, with a calculated ROI of 634.43% and
11,530 conversions.

Instagram was the second-best channel, generating approximately $4.25 million
in revenue from $725.54 thousand spending. Its calculated ROI was 485.22%,
with 8,462 conversions.

Google Ads ranked third with a calculated ROI of 361.50%.

Recommendations:
1. Increase budget allocation toward Email because it achieved the highest
   calculated ROI, highest revenue, highest conversions, and highest conversion
   rate among the channels.
2. Increase Instagram investment gradually because it produced the second-
   highest ROI and strong conversion performance.
3. Maintain Google Ads as a supporting channel because it generated the
   third-highest ROI and substantial conversions.
4. Review lower-performing channels such as YouTube and LinkedIn for
   optimization before increasing their budgets.

Conclusion:
The analysis shows that Email and Instagram provide the strongest combination
of ROI, revenue, and conversions. Therefore, these channels should receive
greater budget priority, while other channels should be optimized and
monitored before additional investment.
